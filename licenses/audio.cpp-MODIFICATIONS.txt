Modifications made by the CAGE project (Apache-2.0 section 4(b)). In summary:
  - the build uses the ggml shared with ik_llama.cpp instead of external/ggml,
    and links into the same shared object as llama.cpp; sources include
    that ggml's headers;
  - attention fixes (flash-attention mask and K/V padding, F16 K/V where
    a backend needs it), multi-device support (spreading a model over
    several devices and reporting what each holds), diagnostics;
  - model loading: weight files are resolved from what is on disk, model
    specs may point at .pt/.pth/.bin files, stricter model-family
    autodetection, OpenF5-TTS loading fixes;
  - fixes needed to build against that ggml after merging upstream.
